### World Spoken Languages Language is an important way people communicate, share ideas, and express their culture. There are thousands of languages spoken around the world, and each language has its own history, traditions, and unique characteristics. Some of the most widely spoken languages include **English, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, Bengali, Portuguese, Russian, and Japanese**. English is widely used as an international language for education, business, travel, and communication. People may speak one or several languages depending on their country, culture, and background. The diversity of languages makes the world culturally rich and helps connect people from different communities.
